### Connecting your AI Analyst to the Business

Series arc: Workshop 1 = "Your first analysis with an AI analyst" → Workshop 3 = connect the analyst to business definitions.

---

### Strategic Framing & Scenario

* **Target Audience:** Mid-to-senior Data Scientists and Analytics Engineers who use ChatGPT/Claude daily, but don't trust Text-to-SQL — hallucinations, bad joins, wrong aggregations.
* **The Business Scenario:** Querying an e-commerce warehouse (Olist, ~100k orders) for net revenue and order metrics across `orders`, `payments`, `items`, and `customers`.
  Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
* **The Core Problem:** Schemas don't carry business meaning. Without that context, Text-to-SQL invents filters, mixes freight into product revenue, and fans out joins until numbers are double-counted.

---

### Storyline

Enterprise challenge (AI can query data but not understand it) → live proof (chat wrong answer) → diagnose on ER slide → intro (optional) → ~40% eval → add business context (semantic layer YAML) → live proof it's fixed (same question + ~90% eval) → where this lives in enterprise → how to structure it → ceiling (bootcamp: guardrails, hooks, evals at scale).

**Win:** audience sees 40% → 90% with one YAML file — they could do this in one day.
**Ceiling:** bootcamp slide last — production needs harnesses on every PR, guardrails, hooks.

Three tool switches: slides → demo → slides (twice). No typing YAML or Python on stage — launcher scripts only.

### Failure Talking Points (from gold_questions.json)

* **Wrong metric:** `SUM(payment_value)` or `SUM(price + freight_value)` for product revenue
* **Fan-out:** `order_payments` × `order_items` in one query (1 order, 2 items, 2 payments → 4 rows → double count)
* **Missing filter:** no `order_status = 'delivered'`

Tiny fan-out example on a whiteboard if needed: 1 order, 2 items, 2 payments → 4 joined rows → revenue × 2.

### How Scoring Works

1. Load each question from `gold_questions.json`
2. `BaseAgent` or `SemanticAgent` generates SQL (live API, `temperature=0`)
3. Execute SQL on `data/olist.duckdb`
4. Compare result rows to `expected_rows` (floats rounded to 2 decimals)
5. **PASS** / **FAIL** per question → `Score: N/10 (N%)`

SQL text is not compared — different SQL with the same correct numbers passes.
